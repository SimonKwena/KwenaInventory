How the sheet must look

  • One worksheet, one row per item per room — an item stored in three rooms is three rows.
  • Required columns: Name, Location, Quantity. Optional: SKU, Description, Category, Subcategory, Quantity Out, Quantity Maintenance, Condition, Status.
  • Location, Condition and Status must match the names on the template's Lists sheet, or the row is reported as an error.
  • Leave the SKU blank and one is generated from the item name; the next sheet with that name adds stock to the same item instead of creating a duplicate.
  • Quantity Out and Quantity Maintenance are optional and default to 0.
  • You get an editable preview of every row — fix any cell, tick the box to drop a row, or add a missing one — and submitting applies the whole sheet in one go, or rolls everything back if anything fails.
